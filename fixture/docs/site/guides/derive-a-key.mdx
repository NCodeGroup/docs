:::tip[Iterations]
600,000 iterations of PBKDF2 with SHA-256 is the figure OWASP recommends; see the
[password storage cheat sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Password_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html).
:::

## Pitfalls

<Pitfalls>

- **Deriving into an array.**
  - *You write* `Rfc2898DeriveBytes.Pbkdf2(password, salt, 600_000, HashAlgorithmName.SHA256, 32)`, which returns a `byte[]`.
  - *What happens:* the key sits in an array the garbage collector may copy, and nothing zeroes it.
  - *Write instead:* derive into the span a vault rents, as the sample above does.

</Pitfalls>

## Checklist

- Derive the key into a vault, never into an array of your own.
- Keep the salt beside the key; it is not a secret.
